# Model card: FraudLens 2026-11-17.1

Generated by `make report` from `docs/results/`. Every number below comes from a committed JSON artefact.

## Intended use

Scores card transactions as they are authorised and decides approve, review or block. Flagged transactions carry reason codes for an analyst. It is a portfolio project on simulated data: it is not validated for real card traffic and must not decide real payments.

## Model

- Arm A3: XGBoost, scale_pos_weight = sqrt(negatives / positives).
- 398 trees, chosen by early stopping on average precision; hyperparameters tuned with Optuna over prequential folds.
- Manifest SHA-256 `49fc5db19b5e23985e71a8778beafa41df8fcffd624fb713981020296e3f97ed`; trained at commit `089e8dcbcc6d`.
- Calibrated with isotonic regression on the late validation slice.

## Data

Sparkov simulated card transactions. Training uses the public training file only, split by time: 960,514 training rows (5,460 frauds), a 11,992-row gap, then 194,501 early and 129,668 late validation rows. Names, addresses and card numbers are removed or tokenised at ingestion; gender is kept only for the fairness check below.

## Features

27 features from one definition shared by training and serving: the amount and its pattern against the card's last 30 days, velocity over 1 hour, 24 hours and 7 days, distance from home and from the previous purchase, novelty of merchant and category, time of day, and cardholder context. Every window reads strictly earlier transactions.

## Calibration and thresholds

- Brier score on the test file: 0.000397 raw, 0.000394 calibrated.
- Review at a calibrated score of 0.0200 or more, block at 0.9333 or more, chosen to minimise expected cost with no more than 1.0% of transactions sent to review.
- Cost assumptions (`configs/costs.yaml`): $4.00 per review, 2% of a wrongly blocked amount, plus $15.00 friction per wrong block. Missed fraud costs its amount.
- Expected cost on the test file: $27.68 per 1,000 transactions, against $2,039.38 for approving everything.
- Decision share on the late validation slice: approve 99.07%, review 0.35%, block 0.59%.

## Limits

- Simulated data. Sparkov's fraud is generated by rules, which is part of why average precision is so high; real fraud adapts.
- Fraud labels are complete and immediate here. In production they arrive weeks late and some are never reported.
- Merchant coordinates in Sparkov are noise around the cardholder's home, so the travel features carry less signal than they would on real data.
- The thresholds depend on cost assumptions; the threshold lab shows how they move.
- Reason codes say why the model scored high, not that fraud happened.
