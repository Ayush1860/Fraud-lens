# Validation report: FraudLens 2026-11-17.1

Generated by `make report`. Organised under the three components of model validation in US supervisory guidance: conceptual soundness, outcomes analysis and ongoing monitoring.

## Conceptual soundness

- **Time-ordered data.** Splits are by time with a gap, features read strictly earlier transactions, and any resampling happens inside the training fold. A property test checks that a later transaction never changes an earlier one's features.
- **One feature definition.** Training and serving call the same function; a parity test compares the batch builder with it on real transactions, and an integration test compares features computed online by the running service with the batch builder.
- **Imbalance handled by evidence.** Seven arms were compared over 5 seeds. The released arm A3 reached average precision 0.990 [0.985, 0.995] on early validation, against 0.436 [0.393, 0.495] for logistic regression.
- **Explanations are exact and checked.** Contributions add up to the margin within 2.5e-05 log-odds on 10,000 rows, and agree with a brute-force Shapley calculation written independently. The top three reason groups are unchanged by a 1% change in amount for 97.8% of 1,000 flagged rows. The largest global contributions: amount, amt_sum_24h, category, is_night, hour.

## Outcomes analysis

The public test file was scored once, by the only function allowed to read it: 555,719 transactions over 194 days.

| Measure | Test |

| Recall at a 1% review budget | 0.944 [0.918, 0.965] |
| Precision at a 1% review budget | 0.358 [0.319, 0.402] |
| Expected cost per 1,000 | $27.68 |

The full tables, the reliability diagram and the review-queue ordering are in [results/report.md](results/report.md). A test re-scores 1,000 stored decisions from their audit rows and reproduces every score exactly.

## Ongoing monitoring

- **Feature drift:** population stability index per feature over the newest 10,000 decisions against the training histograms, refreshed every minute; calendar features are reported but never alarm.
- **Segment drift:** amount within each category, newest window against the previous one; this is what catches a shift confined to one category.
- **Decision mix:** live approve, review and block shares against validation.
- **Explanation drift:** monthly mean absolute contribution per feature, for all rows and for frauds, over 18 months (`results/explainability.json`).
- **Outcomes:** realised precision of labelled alerts, on the console's monitoring page.
- Alarms and what to do about them: [runbook.md](runbook.md).

## Regulatory context

Stated as the project plan's sources summarise it; this is not legal advice.

- United States: Federal Reserve SR 26-2 superseded SR 11-7 on 17 April 2026 ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm)) and keeps conceptual soundness, outcomes analysis and ongoing monitoring as the components of validation.
- India: the RBI's FREE-AI committee report of August 2025 sets seven principles, one of them "Understandable by Design", and names fraud detection as a major use of AI ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scriptS/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=1306)).
- European Union: the AI Act lists credit scoring as high-risk but excludes systems used to detect financial fraud ([Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/)).
